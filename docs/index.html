How it works

  • Type a letter: hold the sign steady until the ring fills (about 0.7 s).
  • J and Z: draw them in the air like normal. The letter is typed when the movement ends.
  • Double letters (the LL in HELLO): drop your hand for a moment, then sign again.
  • End a word: move your hand out of view for 1.5 s, or press Space.

Keep your whole hand in frame, palm toward the camera, about arm's length away, in good light. M, N, S, T, E and D look alike and are the hardest letters.

  • Sign one word at a time. Start with your hands down, sign, then lower your hands (or hold still for a second).
  • Sit far enough back that your face and both hands are in view. Where you sign (chin, forehead, chest) matters.
  • If it picks the wrong word, tap the right guess under the camera to swap it.

Shortcuts: Backspace delete, C clear, S speak all, W switch mode.
