How it works

  • Type a letter: hold the sign steady until the ring fills (about 0.7 s).
  • Double letters (the LL in HELLO): drop your hand for a moment, then sign again.
  • End a word: move your hand out of view for 1.5 s, or press Space.
  • Shortcuts: Backspace delete, C clear, S speak all.

Tips: keep your whole hand in frame, palm toward the camera, about arm's length away, in good light. M, N, S, T, E and D look alike and are the hardest letters. J and Z are motion signs, so the model only sees one frame of them.
